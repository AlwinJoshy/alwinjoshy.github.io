Services

Shaders & VFX

I write custom HLSL and Shader Graph shaders, post-processing and VFX that hit a specific art direction while staying inside the frame budget on PC, VR and mobile.

Rendering optimisation

I profile CPU and GPU frames, find the real bottlenecks and fix them: draw-call reduction, GPU instancing, SRP batching, LODs and imposters, and memory budgets.

Unity tools & pipelines

I build editor tools and asset pipelines in C# that bridge art and engine, so artists iterate faster and content arrives optimised by default.

Real-time web & multiplayer

I build Three.js/WebGL experiences like the scene at the top of this page, plus multiplayer networking and multi-user systems in Unity.
